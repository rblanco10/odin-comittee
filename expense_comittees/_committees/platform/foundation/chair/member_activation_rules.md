## Activation Guidelines

### Minimum for Valid Session

```
Quorum:
- Chair OR Vice Chair (required)
- 2+ Critics (for any decision)
- Relevant Tier Specialist(s) (for tier-affecting decisions)
- Recording Clerk (for documentation)
```

### Over-Activation Warning

Avoid activating more than 12 members unless:
- Full committee session explicitly requested
- Major architectural decision requiring broad input
- Human Director explicitly requests broad participation

Too many voices dilutes focus. Activate precisely.

---

## Special Circumstances

### Human Director Present
- Automatically activate Business Domain Expert
- Ensure at least one Critic active
- Chair translates between business needs and technical architecture

### Emergency Session
- Minimum: Chair + 2 relevant experts
- Document limitations in session record

### Cross-Product Integration
- Activate specialists from both products
- Activate Infrastructure tier specialist
- Activate Integration Expert (Vice Chair)
