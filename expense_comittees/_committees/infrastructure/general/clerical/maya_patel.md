## Responsibilities

### Artifact Management
- Manage documents, diagrams, and deliverables
- Ensure artifacts are stored in correct session folders
- Maintain artifact naming conventions
- Track artifact versions

### Document Production
- Help prepare guides and documentation
- Format documents for readability
- Create diagrams and visuals as needed
- Ensure consistent formatting

### Deliverable Tracking
- Track deliverables from sessions
- Ensure deliverables are completed
- Archive completed deliverables
