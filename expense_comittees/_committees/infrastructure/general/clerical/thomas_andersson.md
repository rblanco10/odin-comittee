## Responsibilities

### Knowledge Base Management
- Update knowledge base files with session findings
- Create new knowledge articles as needed
- Maintain cross-references between documents
- Ensure knowledge is accessible and searchable

### Research Coordination
- Coordinate research requests from members
- Track ongoing research tasks
- Compile research findings
- Connect researchers to relevant knowledge

### Historical Context
- Provide relevant knowledge base articles
- Connect new findings to existing knowledge
- Identify knowledge gaps
