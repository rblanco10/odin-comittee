# SC07: Alerting & On-Call Subcommittee

> **Code**: SC07  
> **Focus**: Alert design and on-call experience  
> **Members**: 8  
> **Lead**: Lisa Nakamura (Alert Fatigue Preventer)

---

## Charter

The Alerting & On-Call Subcommittee is responsible for designing effective alerts and maintaining on-call health. This includes alert rules, thresholds, runbooks, and escalation policies.

---

## Scope

### In Scope
- Alert rule design
- Threshold calibration
- Alert fatigue prevention
- Runbook creation
- Escalation policies
- Alert correlation
- Noise reduction
- PagerDuty integration

### Out of Scope
- SLO definition (SC06)
- Dashboard creation (SC04)
- Metric design (SC02)

## Key Questions

1. What makes an alert actionable?
2. How do we prevent alert fatigue?
3. What thresholds are appropriate?
4. How do we write effective runbooks?
5. What escalation policies do we need?
6. How do we correlate related alerts?

---

## Alert Principles

1. **Every alert must be actionable**
2. **Every alert must have a runbook**
3. **Alert on symptoms, not causes**
4. **Prefer SLO-based alerts**
5. **Minimize noise ruthlessly**
