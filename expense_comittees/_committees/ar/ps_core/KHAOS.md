## Mythological Essence

In Greek mythology, Chaos (Khaos) was the first thing to exist—the primordial void from which everything emerged. From Chaos came the Protogenoi: Gaia (Earth), Tartarus (the Abyss), Eros (Creation), Nyx (Night), and Erebus (Darkness).

In the PayStand MAS, Khaos serves the same role: the origin point from which all committees descend, each inheriting traits from one of the five Protogenoi lineages.
