## 1. The Mission
To **reboot commercial finance** by creating an open financial system. We move businesses away from fee-heavy, archaic banking rails toward a zero-fee, blockchain-verified network.

---

## 2. Core Products

### Accounts Receivable (AR)
*   **Goal**: Automate "Money In".
*   **Features**: Smart Invoices, Payment Portal, Auto-Reconciliation, Collections.
*   **Key Concept**: **Payer Incentives**. Moving payers from Credit Cards (High Fee) to Bank Rails (Zero Fee) by passing fees or offering discounts.

### Accounts Payable (AP)
*   **Goal**: Automate "Money Out".
*   **Features**: Invoice Processing, Vendor Management, PO Matching, Bulk Payments.
*   **Key Concept**: **DeFi Cards**. Corporate cards that earn yield or rewards, integrated directly into the payment flow.

### Payments-as-a-Service
*   **The Rails**:
    *   **PayStand Bank Network (Zero Fee)**: Direct bank-to-bank transfers (eCheck/ACH/RTP).
    *   **Card Networks**: Visa/Mastercard/Amex (High Fee).
    *   **Blockchain**: The immutable ledger verifying every transaction.
*   **Smart Lockbox**: Digitizing paper checks.

---

## 3. The Blockchain Difference
PayStand is **SaaS on Blockchain**.
*   **Immutable Audit Trail**: Every transaction is hashed and recorded.
*   **Trust**: No need for third-party verification; the chain proves the event.
*   **Efficiency**: Smart Contracts automate the flow of funds and reconciliation.

## 5. Financial Regulations (The Laws)
*   **PCI-DSS**: We handle cards, so security is paramount. Tokenization is mandatory.
*   **Money Transmission**: We move money. State licenses (MTL) and federal registration (FinCEN) apply.
*   **KYC/KYB**: Know Your Customer/Business. We must verify who is using our platform.
*   **OFAC**: We cannot process payments for sanctioned entities.
