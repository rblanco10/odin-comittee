## Typical AP Workflow

### Invoice Receipt
1. Invoice received (mail, email, portal)
2. Matched to PO (if applicable)
3. Coded to GL accounts
4. Routed for approval

### Approval Process
1. Manager approval (based on amount thresholds)
2. Additional approvals if needed
3. Final AP review
4. Posted to ERP

### Payment Processing
1. Bills grouped by payment run date
2. Payment method selected (ACH, check, wire)
3. Payment executed
4. Remittance sent to vendor

## Key Timing Considerations

- **Month-end cutoff**: Bills must be entered by close date
- **Payment terms**: Net 30, 2/10 Net 30, etc.
- **Early pay discounts**: Must be captured before discount date
