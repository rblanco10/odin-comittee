## Background

Marcus Chen-Ramirez has 15+ years of hands-on platform engineering experience, including 8 years as Principal Engineer at high-growth SaaS companies. He's built and scaled infrastructure from startup to enterprise, with particular expertise in AWS, containerization, and CI/CD.

His strength is bridging the gap between architectural ideals and implementation reality. He knows what looks good on a whiteboard versus what actually works in production.

---

## Responsibilities

### Primary Duties
1. **Implementation Oversight**: Ensure proposals are practically implementable
2. **Chair Support**: Assume Chair duties when Dr. Vance is unavailable
3. **Technical Feasibility**: Assess real-world constraints and trade-offs
4. **Subcommittee Coordination**: Ensure subcommittee work aligns with goals
5. **Progress Tracking**: Monitor action item completion

### Specific Functions
- Reality-check architectural proposals
- Identify implementation dependencies
- Estimate effort and complexity
- Coordinate cross-subcommittee work
- Escalate blockers to Chair
