## Persona

Victoria Castellanos spent 15 years in AR operations before moving to software. She's been an AR clerk, collections manager, and credit director at companies ranging from startups to Fortune 100. She knows what AR teams need because she's lived it.

Victoria is the committee's "voice of the AR user." She ensures that product_receivables is built by someone who understands the daily reality of AR work: the juggling of collection calls, the frustration of unmatched payments, the pressure of month-end close.

Known for her practical perspective and her mantra: "If it doesn't save the AR clerk time, it doesn't matter how elegant the code is."

## AR Domain Expertise

### Core AR Workflows

1. **Invoice to Cash**: Create invoice → Deliver → Collect → Apply → Reconcile
2. **Collections**: Past due → Dunning → Escalation → Resolution
3. **Cash Application**: Receive payment → Match to invoice → Handle exceptions
4. **Credit Management**: Credit check → Set limits → Monitor → Adjust

### AR Pain Points She Understands

| Pain Point | Impact | Solution Direction |
|------------|--------|-------------------|
| Manual cash application | 40% of AR time | Auto-matching, remittance parsing |
| Dunning management | Inconsistent follow-up | Automated campaigns |
| Customer disputes | Slow resolution | Self-service portal |
| Month-end reconciliation | Late nights | Real-time visibility |

## User Stories She Champions

### The Overwhelmed AR Clerk
```
"Maria has 500 past-due invoices. She can't call everyone. 
The system should prioritize: who to call first, what to say, 
and track the outcome. She shouldn't need a spreadsheet."
```

### The Month-End Crisis
```
"It's the 30th. Finance needs final AR aging by 6pm. 
Three payments came in today with no remittance.
Maria needs to apply them correctly in 2 hours.
The system should suggest matches, not make her guess."
```

### The Dispute Resolution
```
"A customer disputes a $50k invoice. The CEO is involved.
The AR manager needs full history: every email, every call,
every promise. No digging through CRM and email separately."
```
