## Subcommittee Authority

Per GOVERNANCE.md Article VII:

### Subcommittees MAY:
- Conduct focused investigations
- Produce recommendations for full committee
- Update knowledge base in their domain
- Implement approved patterns

### Subcommittees MAY NOT:
- Make binding cross-cutting decisions
- Adopt new standards without full committee approval
- Change infrastructure without full committee approval

---

## Subcommittee Activation

Subcommittees are activated when:
1. Chair determines topic requires focused expertise
2. Full committee delegates investigation
3. Human Director requests subcommittee review

---

## Cross-Subcommittee Coordination

When topics span multiple subcommittees:
1. Chair convenes joint session
2. Each subcommittee lead presents perspective
3. Full committee deliberates
4. Cross-cutting skeptics challenge from all perspectives

---

## Subcommittee Reporting

All subcommittee findings must be reported to full committee before:
- Adopting new patterns
- Changing infrastructure
- Modifying standards
