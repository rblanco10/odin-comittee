# Domain Terms Glossary

---

## Payment Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Payout** | Outbound payment from us to recipient |
| **PayoutBatch** | Group of payouts processed together |
| **PayoutItem** | Single payout within a batch |
| **Payment Method** | How payment is delivered (ACH, check, card) |
| **Payment Rails** | Infrastructure for moving money (ACH network, card networks) |
| **Settlement** | Final transfer of funds between parties |

---

## Card Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **CardIssuance** | Record of an issued card |
| **Card Token** | Provider's identifier for a card |
| **PAN** | Primary Account Number (full card number) |
| **CVV/CVC** | Card Verification Value/Code |
| **MCC** | Merchant Category Code (type of business) |
| **Authorization** | Real-time approval of card transaction |
| **Clearing** | Settlement of card transaction |

---

## Identity Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **KYB** | Know Your Business - business verification |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer - individual verification |
| **Beneficial Owner** | Individual owning 25%+ of business |
| **Control Person** | Individual with significant control |
| **CIP** | Customer Identification Program |

---

## Technical Terms

| Term | Definition |
|------|------------|
| **Capability** | Abstract operation providers can perform |
| **Adapter** | Provider-specific implementation |
| **Reactor** | Multi-step workflow |
| **Webhook** | Event notification from provider |
| **Workspace** | Tenant/organization unit |

---

*"Clear terminology enables clear communication."*
